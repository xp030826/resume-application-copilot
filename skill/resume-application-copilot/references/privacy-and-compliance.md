# Privacy and Compliance

## Data tiers

- Standard: name, contact channels, public portfolio links, education, verified experience, skills, target role, and availability.
- Sensitive: birth date, gender, political status, precise address, salary history, family relationships, visa status, and background-check answers. Leave unchecked by default.
- Restricted: national ID, passport number, bank details, health or disability data, emergency contacts, reference contacts, passwords, verification codes, and biometrics. Exclude unless explicitly authorized for a named destination.

## Required boundaries

- Store real profile data locally and encrypt browser vaults with a master password.
- Never commit resumes, profiles, logs, keys, or vault exports.
- Do not send personal data to an AI service without explicit disclosure and consent.
- Do not bypass login, captcha, anti-bot checks, rate limits, or platform restrictions.
- Do not click final submit, send recruiter messages, or accept declarations automatically.
- Require manual review for truthfulness, conflicts, health, criminal history, and work authorization declarations.

## Provenance

Keep source file names and last-verification dates for claims that affect eligibility or credibility. Mark inferred values as proposals until confirmed.
