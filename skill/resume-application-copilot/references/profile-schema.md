# Candidate Profile Schema

Use direct values for confirmed facts. During intake, keep value, source, confidence, and last_verified metadata for uncertain or changing claims.

## Account and privacy

Job-search status, visibility, blocked companies, anonymous browsing, backup contacts, account bindings, and notification preferences.

## Personal and legal

Names, pronouns, gender, birth date, phone, email, address, city/district, hometown, household registration, political status, nationality, work authorization, visa, military status, travel/relocation/remote preferences, public profiles, GitHub, and portfolio links.

## Job intent

Role titles and category leaves, industries, locations, employment type, campus/social hiring, availability, internship length/days, and monthly/annual compensation.

## Education

Institution, department, major, degree and degree type, dates, GPA, average score, ranking, courses, thesis, supervisor, research direction, and restricted verification identifiers.

## Employment and internships

Employer, industry, size, funding stage, department, title, supervisor level, dates, location, responsibilities, quantified achievements, leaving reason, and sensitive reference details.

## Projects and research

Name, dates, background, role, team size, architecture/workflow, personal contribution, measured results, links, publications, patents, software copyrights, and awards.

## Skills and student experience

Truthful proficiency, certificates, languages, driving licences, student leadership, organizations, volunteering, scholarships, competitions, honours, and thesis/design.

## Attachments and reusable answers

Resume variants, cover letters, transcripts, certificates, identity/student documents, portfolios, recommendations, self-introduction, motivation, strengths, weaknesses, career plan, and role-specific open-question answers.

## Risk and declarations

Relatives/conflicts, non-compete obligations, background-check consent, legally appropriate health/disability/criminal-history questions, and truthfulness/privacy declarations. Always require manual review.
